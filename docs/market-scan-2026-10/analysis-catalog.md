| 31 | Hill et al. 2025, "LLMs for Thematic Analysis in Healthcare Research: a blinded mixed-methods comparison" (medRxiv) | https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.25.25343031.full.pdf | Preprint (full text read) | Verbatim quote and segment ID required per code. Strict hallucination rate 1.2%, but **comprehensive error rate 12.4%, mostly from LLMs coding facilitator speech**. LLMs non-inferior for deductive coding, weaker for inductive and latent meaning. Low code prevalence gives κ 0.34 even at 93% agreement, so Gwet's AC1 is used. | yes | 5 |

Theme definition covers central concept, boundaries, uniqueness, and contribution to the RQ. Pipelines that omitted interviews were excluded. | yes | 5 |
